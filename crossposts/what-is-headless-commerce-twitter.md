**Tweet 2:**
Traditional e-commerce = your storefront and backend are bolted together.

Every app you install, every widget you add, every customization makes your store slower.

Headless = you separate the two. Keep your Shopify backend. Replace the storefront with custom code.

**Tweet 3:**
The numbers from a real migration:

Before (Shopify theme):
- 3.8s load time
- 42/100 PageSpeed
- 54% bounce rate

After (Next.js + Shopify API):
- 0.9s load time
- 97/100 PageSpeed
- 24% bounce rate

Conversion went from 1.8% to 2.9%.

**Tweet 4:**
"But I'll lose all my products and orders!"

No. Headless only replaces the frontend.

Your products, customers, orders, payment setup stay in Shopify. The new storefront connects through API.

Same admin panel. Same workflow. Just 4x faster for customers.

**Tweet 5:**
Who DOESN'T need headless:

- Happy with current setup, not looking to grow
- Think custom sites need a dev for every change (they don't, CMS dashboards handle it)
- Happy with current traffic and conversions
- Store converts well on mobile

If everything is working for you, keep it.

**Tweet 6:**
Who DOES need headless:

- PageSpeed under 50 on mobile
- Conversion rate has plateaued
- Need custom checkout/product experience
- Selling across multiple channels
- Store does $500K+/year in revenue

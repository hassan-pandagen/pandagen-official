Direction C · chosen

Annotated paper.

Direction A's paper and type, plus one teaching device: numbered cognac markers and short notes that point at the exact part of a real screenshot the voice is talking about. Charcoal is kept for the end card only, the way the site keeps it for the contact block.

Strength
Every screenshot gets a reason to be on screen. Crops stay readable on a phone because the marker tells the camera where to go. Works the same for a website, a workflow or an editor, so six films can share it without looking identical.
Risk
Annotation can turn into clutter. Rule: one marker at a time, one note of eight words or fewer.

Decision

C, annotated paper. The campaign is an explainer: each film teaches one idea using real screens. A shows the screens without saying what to look at; B is striking but fights the light screenshots and the cream site the viewer lands on. C keeps A's brand fidelity and adds the one device an explainer needs, a pointer. It also gives the "Example workflow" and "Example journey" labels a consistent, visible home.

## Format notes

- **16:9 master.** Left-hand headline column, right-hand page, phone or screenshot. S5 uses the full width for the homepage.
- **9:16 social (priority).** Recomposed, not cropped. Essential text stays inside y 270–1248 and x ≤ 930 px, keeping the top 14 %, the bottom 35 % and the right-hand action rail clear (Meta Reels guidance, the strictest of the main platforms). The example page is a phone from frame one, so there is no desktop-to-phone morph. Screenshot windows stop above the caption band (y 630–1090), crops are tighter, and the homepage uses the real mobile capture. Founder cards and a SCOPE AND PRICE anchor take the headline slot.
- **1:1 square.** Headline on top, visual below, windows stopping above the captions. The homepage beat uses the mobile capture. Founder cards move into the headline slot.
- **Reduced motion (9:16).** Same beats and timings. Moves land at their start time with a fade of at least 0.45 s, camera framings cross-fade instead of travelling, the end card fades instead of wiping, and tap rings barely grow.

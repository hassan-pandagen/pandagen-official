## Before you change anything

Open the profile and read what is there now. Change it only if it does one of these:

- sells **migration as the whole offer** ("SEO-safe website migrations", "we move
  you off WordPress") rather than as one route in
- quotes a **price or scope we have corrected**: any store at $5,000, "no retainer",
  "no monthly platform fees", hosting "from $20 a month", "built in 10 weeks",
  "95% process automation", a 9.0/10 rating
- names **Stripe or PayPal** as something we run on
- describes us as a **Next.js or migration specialist** only

If it is already broad and accurate, leave it. A profile that does not contradict
the site does not need editing.

**Do not touch** reviews, ratings or anything a third party wrote. Do not add an
aggregate rating anywhere: `company-facts.ts` excludes those deliberately because
we cannot support one.

## Fields that are not copy, and are worth checking once

- **Website URL** resolves to `https://www.pandacodegen.com` with no redirect chain.
- **Name** is spelled `PandaCodeGen`, one word. Some directories hold "Panda Code Gen";
  the site declares that as an `alternateName`, so either is fine, but pick one and
  keep it.
- **Legal name** is Panda Group Ventures L.L.C. where a profile asks separately.
- **Address** is 12250 S Kirkwood Rd, Apt 1128, Stafford, TX 77477, United States, and
  the phone is +1 (302) 773-8982. These two have to match the site character for
  character or they stop corroborating the business and start confusing it.
- **Founded**: the site distinguishes trading since February 2026 from the LLC formed
  12 August 2026. If a profile has one date field, use the LLC date, which is the one
  a reviewer is matching against a filing.

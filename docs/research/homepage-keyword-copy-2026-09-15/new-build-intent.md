# New website, redesign and ecommerce search intent

The homepage should clearly include both a first website and a replacement website. The sampled results support **website design and development** as the broad commercial category, **website redesign services** for an existing-site problem, and **ecommerce website development** for a store project. “New website” is useful language for a visitor to recognise their situation. This research does not establish it as the highest-demand keyword.

Research conducted on 15 September 2026 using fresh external searches and primary service pages. No Google Search Console data was used. The search tool did not expose a controlled country, device, personalisation state or complete ranked Google result set. US and UK modifiers were used in selected queries, and the provider pages below describe serving those markets; this is not a country-specific rank or search-volume export. No volume, keyword difficulty, CPC or ranking forecast is asserted.

## What the wording signals

| Term family | Observed destination / interpretation | Recommended role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website design services | Broad commercial services: business website design, planning, user experience and sometimes development. The category also includes providers working on their own website builders. | Use natural category wording on the homepage, such as “Website design & development”. Explain that PandaCodeGen builds and hands over the actual site. |
| Custom website development | Bespoke builds with functional requirements; sampled UK results also include web apps. “Development” can attract a more technical brief than “design”. | Include in the homepage summary and relevant service introduction. Keep the first customer explanation in ordinary business language. |
| Small business website design | A clearly named audience segment. Sampled pages focus on first sites or replacing dated sites, understandable services, trust and contact paths. | Use if this is a deliberate audience choice. It should not become the main H1 merely to collect an additional phrase if the business also wants midsize buyers. |
| New website design / new website | The unqualified phrase produced a mixed sample including a first-party how-to guide. Service pages often express the situation as creating a site from scratch or launching a business website. | Say explicitly “We build new websites…” in the opening copy. Do not create a separate “new website design” page based only on this sample. |
| Website redesign services | Commercial replacement/improvement intent. Existing sites, brand changes, user journeys, design and launch are central. | Strong service-section and service-page language. Include “redesign” naturally on the homepage to reassure existing-site buyers. |
| Website rebuild services | Real commercial usage in the UK sample, often covering content, URLs, performance and structural changes alongside redesign. | A useful plain-language verb for what PandaCodeGen does. Treat it as overlapping vocabulary within the redesign/migration offering unless later evidence establishes a distinct destination need. |
| Ecommerce website development | Commercial store work covering products, checkout, payments, integrations and sometimes replatforming. | Use the service card and existing ecommerce destination. “Online stores” can stay in the hero because it is immediately understandable. |

These are intent and page-assignment recommendations, not a ranking of keyword demand.

## Primary page observations

**WebFX: redesign has a commercial destination of its own.** Its [website redesign service page](https://www.webfx.com/web-design/services/redesign/) leads with the redesign category and business performance, then offers a proposal, portfolio, estimate and delivery information. It covers both design and development. Platform experience appears further into the service explanation. This demonstrates a way to target redesign without putting every supported platform into the opening sentence. It does not prove that the layout caused conversions or that PandaCodeGen can claim WebFX’s outcomes.

**Forwardify: rebuild and redesign overlap.** Its [UK website rebuild page](https://www.forwardify.com/digital-services/website-rebuilds/) combines rebuild and redesign language, describes an audit of what should stay or change, and discusses URLs, redirects, content migration, performance and enquiry journeys. It is a useful example of explaining a rebuild in terms a business owner can recognise. The page does not establish that “rebuild” has more search demand than “redesign”.

**DPOM: bespoke website work can contain technical depth below a broad offer.** Its [website development page](https://www.dpom.co.uk/website-development/) opens with custom websites and web apps, then discusses its own CMS, editing and specific functional needs such as quoting or booking. The lesson is structural: identify the product, then explain the system. Its proprietary CMS offer differs from PandaCodeGen’s stack and ownership arrangements, so its product promises should not be copied.

**Webteqno: a first website and a replacement fit under one service.** Its [small business website design page](https://webteqno.com/small-business-website-design) explicitly addresses businesses launching their first substantial site or replacing an old one. It connects the category to explaining the business, showing proof and giving visitors a next step. This supports inclusive customer wording without treating new-build and redesign prospects as unrelated businesses.

**Maven Peak: store work includes both launching and migrating.** The opened [ecommerce website development page](https://www.mavenpeaksolutions.com/services/ecommerce-website-development) describes new ecommerce projects, legacy-store migration, payments and operational integrations. Platform options appear in the supporting content. Its search snippet and opened page had different wording, so the opened page was used for these observations. This is evidence of the category’s breadth, not independent validation of the provider’s performance, qualifications or sales claims.

For the ambiguity of “new website design”, a [Webflow first-party how-to guide](https://webflow.com/blog/how-to-design-a-website) appeared in the unqualified search sample. That supports caution about assuming exclusively purchase-ready intent; it does not establish the proportion of informational versus commercial searches.

## Wording recommendation for the parent copy review

Keep the main headline focused on the business website offer. Add one direct supporting sentence that answers both situations:

> We build new websites and online stores, and redesign or rebuild existing sites around what your business needs next.

“Redesign” communicates the recognised service category. “Rebuild” helps explain that the work can go beyond a cosmetic refresh. In the next sentence or lower service section, say what actually comes across when moving a current site: agreed content, URLs and integrations. Do not imply that every migration requires a redesign, or that every redesign requires a platform change.

For a short section below the work, a heading such as **“Starting fresh or replacing your current website?”** makes the two buying situations explicit. The paragraph can then describe design, development and migration in normal prose, followed by useful links to the actual services.

The objective is not to fit every service keyword into one paragraph. The homepage introduces the offer; the relevant service pages supply the detail a buyer needs for each kind of project. Repeating “new website design”, “website rebuild services” and “website redesign services” as three separate offers would add wording without necessarily adding a meaningful choice.

# Homepage keyword language and summary copy

**Use the homepage to introduce new websites, online stores and existing-site redesigns. Explain migration as the work needed to move agreed content, URLs and connected systems. Put platform names in a useful service section below that introduction.** This covers the main buying situations without making the H1 a catalogue of platforms or assuming every visitor already owns a website.

The recommended vocabulary is website design and development, redesign, rebuild and migration. Avoid **convert your website** as the main service label: the destination is unclear, and the sampled results included app/file conversion and conversion-rate optimisation. The recommendation concerns the meaning of the offer, not a claim that a particular sentence has proven search volume or conversion performance.

## Evidence and limits

The assessment combines fresh external searches and official provider pages checked on 15 September 2026 with preserved DataForSEO records. No Search Console queries were used to infer market demand. A new keyword-volume API was not available in this session. The numerical evidence is therefore dated, while the search-intent observations are new. The public search sample is not a controlled US or UK Google ranking export.

The [preserved-data reconciliation](preserved-keyword-data.md) distinguishes two records: a 13 September reconstruction written on 14 September, and a separate contemporaneous 14 September capture. The latter contains 16 terms; it does not refresh every earlier query. Its monthly arrays end in February 2026, so a recent retrieval date does not establish recent underlying demand.

The [new-build and redesign assessment](new-build-intent.md) and [migration assessment](migration-intent.md) record the searches, primary pages and meaning of each term family. Their findings establish examples of commercial or competing intent, not how frequently people search or how readily PandaCodeGen can rank.

## What the fresh searches clarify

**New build and redesign fit under one broad category.** Webteqno's small-business service explicitly addresses both first websites and replacements. WebFX has a distinct redesign service, while Forwardify combines rebuild and redesign on its UK service page. These examples support a broad introduction followed by relevant detail. They do not establish that separate pages for every synonym would improve visibility. [Webteqno](https://webteqno.com/small-business-website-design), [WebFX redesign](https://www.webfx.com/web-design/services/redesign/), [Forwardify rebuilds](https://www.forwardify.com/digital-services/website-rebuilds/)

**Migration alone is ambiguous.** Kinsta's WordPress migration service can move a WordPress site between hosts while keeping WordPress. That buyer may need a much smaller job than a custom rebuild. Describe what PandaCodeGen is moving and what the replacement is. [Kinsta migration](https://kinsta.com/wordpress-hosting/migration/)

**Platform plus redesign does not necessarily mean leaving that platform.** Wix's own refurbishment guidance includes options that stay within Wix. If a service page offers a custom replacement, the surrounding wording should make that direction clear. [Wix site refurbishment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/revamping-your-site-5984263)

**Conversion can describe a different purchase.** WebFX's conversion-rate service concerns visitor actions and funnel improvements. Broad conversion queries also returned other meanings in the sampled results. Use redesign/rebuild for replacement-site work; reserve conversion language for a clearly stated conversion-rate or technical-conversion task. [WebFX CRO](https://www.webfx.com/digital-marketing/services/conversion-rate-optimization/)

**A custom storefront can retain Shopify.** Shopify's official documentation supports a separately built storefront using its commerce APIs. Consequently a list of all supported platforms should not be introduced as platforms that every client must leave. The repository also documents a GoHighLevel route that may retain its CRM. [Shopify headless documentation](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/bring-your-own-stack), [GoHighLevel service source](../../../src/app/services/gohighlevel/PageContent.tsx)

## Recommended section

Use this as the short explanation below the broad hero. It replaces the current dense summary; it should not be appended to it.

> **New websites, redesigns and migrations.**
>
> PandaCodeGen designs and builds fast business websites and online stores. We create new websites and redesign or rebuild existing sites around how your business works. When a project needs a migration, we plan how your content, URLs and integrations carry across. You get a content editor, training and code you own.

This paragraph names both starting situations, the commercial service categories, performance as a build objective and practical handover benefits. It does not claim that changing a platform guarantees a faster site or preserved rankings. The existing hero can retain its accepted starting-price line rather than making this paragraph repeat every commercial term.

For the platform subsection lower in the service area:

> **Options for your current website.**
>
> We rebuild sites from WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and Webflow as custom websites, with the content and connections to keep agreed before work starts. For Shopify, WooCommerce and GoHighLevel, we assess what should stay and what needs to change.

Put relevant service links under that explanation, with labels that describe the work. Keep this subsection in the existing service area; do not add another large block before the first projects. The [copy sheet](recommended-section.md) separates public wording from the route map and editing notes.

## Placement and search relevance

Google says its language-matching systems can relate pages to queries even when the exact terms are absent. This supports natural wording rather than repeating every combination of new website, website rebuild, website redesign and migration. A complete sentence does not need its own reported keyword volume to help the page explain the offer. [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)

Google's link guidance recommends descriptive, concise labels with surrounding context. Platform names should help a visitor choose a relevant route. Use labelled links or small service cards beneath the explanation, rather than chaining many repeated keyword phrases into the summary paragraph. [Google link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable)

There is no need to put every platform into the H1. Nor is there a reason to hide those names from buyers who need them. Their useful role is to identify applicable work in context. Google's spam guidance addresses unnatural keyword repetition; it does not establish that a short, relevant list of supported platforms is inherently problematic. [Google keyword-stuffing guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#keyword-stuffing)

Keep the page's broad title and category, then use one concise summary, early work and deeper service explanations. Copy changes should serve a reader's decision. They are not a guarantee of rankings, citations or lower bounce.

## What to leave out of this summary

The current screenshot's universal two-to-four-week timeline, no-monthly-platform-fees wording and no-retainer wording do not describe every current offer. Hosting and retained commerce services may have running costs; ongoing services are optional, not nonexistent. Performance targets belong with their agreed test conditions. These terms can be explained on the relevant pricing and service pages instead of loading the summary with qualifications. [Current company facts](../../../src/data/company-facts.ts), [earlier consistency audit](../homepage-positioning-2026-09-15/follow-up-audit.md)

The paragraph also need not name Next.js, every AI assistant and a Lighthouse score to explain what the business sells. Those details remain useful in delivery and technical sections. The goal is complete coverage of the main customer situations, not every fact in the company record.

## Scope of this recommendation

This research provides a short replacement section and a platform-link map. It does not apply changes to the application, resolve the separate pricing-quiz contradictions or claim a new keyword-volume capture. The earlier findings remain documented rather than being implicitly marked complete.

If further keyword investment is planned, collect separate US and UK estimates with a named reporting period, monthly breakdown and original response preserved. For the present wording decision, use the supported service categories and clear new-versus-existing-site distinction. Do not postpone an accurate explanation simply because an exact conversational sentence has not been measured as a query.

## What we can and cannot count

[Google Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en) supplies monthly search estimates when accessible through a Google Ads account. [Google’s metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) explain that averages depend on the selected location, network and time window and include close variants. Advertiser competition is not an organic keyword-difficulty score. A high CPC is not evidence that a small agency can rank easily.

[The Google Ads historical-metrics API](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/keyword-planning/generate-historical-metrics) can combine closely related submitted terms in one result. Record the returned close variants before adding anything together. Do not sum “CMS development company” and “CMS development services” blindly, or assume a displayed zero means nobody searches for the service.

No authenticated Keyword Planner, seodata.dev or equivalent volume tool is available in the current connected tool inventory. A check of relevant credential *names* found no existing matching local integration; no credential values were displayed. A question is pending about existing account access. The previous CMS estimates in the conversation are historical claims awaiting source, geography, time-window and grouping verification.

[Bing AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/) reports a publisher’s citations and grounding phrases across supported surfaces. [The expanded insights](https://blogs.bing.com/search/2026/6/New-AI-Visibility-Insights-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Intents-Topics-Citation-Share-Compare/) add topic/intent and citation-share comparisons. These are useful exposure signals, not the total number of US consumers who asked a question across all AI products. Repeated prompts in an AI visibility tool measure results for the chosen sample, not population-wide monthly demand.

Consequently, Google volume, observed AI citations and sampled AI answer inclusion must be separate columns. A figure labelled “AI searches per month” needs a named provider, sampling method, coverage and time period; none has been established here.

## How to decide after obtaining numbers

Use US, English and Google Search only for the initial export; record the provider, capture date and underlying months. Include close variants, monthly trend and the source’s advertiser competition/CPC fields, with their limitations. Keep organic difficulty and ranking observations separately sourced. Compare target queries with existing page coverage before choosing new content.

Prioritize a query when it matches work PandaCodeGen actually delivers, has a suitable existing destination and can be answered with distinctive evidence. More searches do not automatically make a query better. A generic definition may produce less hiring intent than a narrow migration or editing problem. Current keyword changes should preserve the useful migration and vendor coverage rather than abandon it for an untested broad phrase.

[Google’s AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) supports indexable, accessible content and ordinary SEO fundamentals; special AI markup is not required. For citation usefulness, answer the question directly, name the relevant platform and conditions, cite the primary source for export/integration limits and show genuine project evidence. Do not promise inclusion in AI answers or manufacture a special section solely for machines.

[Google's definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) describe advertiser competition and monthly estimates including close variants. Zero CPC is a reported zero, not proof a buyer has no value or ads are free.
- Do not sum related variants or their displayed volumes. Treat seodata.dev's SERP-overlap score as a provider-reported grouping hint; no live US result overlap was independently measured here. Identical overlap or volume does not prove two phrases are interchangeable for every buyer. The site's Google Ads sourcing statement is a provider assertion; the pasted output does not expose its upstream query, month range or close-variant grouping.
- These are Google keyword estimates, including phrases about AI. They do not measure how many prompts people submit to ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity.

# Editorial, showcase and resource backlink prospects

Researched 16 September 2026. This is public-web discovery, not an Ahrefs/Semrush backlink-index export. A verified entry below means the source page exposes an outbound link to the named competitor or its project. It does **not** establish that the link is followed, indexed, independent editorial endorsement, or a source of rankings/leads. Link `rel` attributes were not audited. No messages, accounts, purchases or submissions were made.

The useful distinction is between a real competing backlink and an immediately sensible opportunity. There are nine verified examples below, but several require a new useful asset, a selective editorial decision, a reopened submission program, or a paid participation budget. Do not turn all nine into an outreach queue.

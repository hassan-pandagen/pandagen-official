## Follow-up and handling

- No automatic sequence is active. If an eligible first message is sent, wait at least seven business days before considering one relevant follow-up; check for replies first. Stop after a decline, opt-out or bounce. Never follow up just because an application has no link yet.
- Use ordinary branded URLs. Do not add tracking pixels or ask publishers to change editorial link attributes. Track responses and published links in the ledger, and referral enquiries in the existing analytics where observable.
- Site/application access, a publisher's acceptance, and an actual live backlink are separate states. Save the submission receipt or message ID; later capture the published page and destination before reporting a link as acquired.
- A founder's public email/contact address is a communication route, not evidence of consent to a bulk campaign. Keep each message specific to the recipient's published program or existing citation.

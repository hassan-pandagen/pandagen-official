## Approach

Lead with useful existing experience, not a request to do the agency a favor. Offer one concrete lesson, a short interview or an original worked example. Do not promise new assets, deadlines, revenue outcomes or client data access. Do not buy links, ask for exact-match anchors or count sent messages as placements. Major-vendor applications are now secondary, not the immediate campaign focus.

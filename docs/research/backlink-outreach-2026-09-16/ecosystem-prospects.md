## Requirements that affect the decision

- **Vercel listing vs certification:** The partner application is open to agencies, consultancies and integrators, with review before onboarding. A separate [certification announcement](https://vercel.com/blog/vercel-launches-partner-certification) describes platform, Next.js and AI Cloud knowledge and a minimum number of certified developers, without giving the number there. Do not equate an application or a directory tier with having certification.
- **Netlify changed its program:** Use the [current program](https://www.netlify.com/partners/), not the 2020 or 2022 agency announcements as current eligibility rules. Certified Partners must be active Netlify customers, be in good standing as Ecosystem Partners, complete training, and refer at least three new Pro customers/year or one Enterprise customer/year. Whether entry-tier acceptance yields a public website link remains unknown.
- **Shopify's current Plus gate:** [Official requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/grow-your-business/partner-directory/join) specify at least $500,000 annual Shopify referral/co-sell revenue **or** $2.5 million annual Shopify revenue from qualifying existing merchants; also at least two Plus/Enterprise shops **or** 40 Standard shops in the last five years. Credential requirements are ten Verified Skills across the organization and at least three people with a skill. These are Shopify revenue metrics, not the agency's own revenue. Plus or above guarantees directory eligibility; limited Registered invitations remain possible.
- **Webflow is a business-model choice:** [Current application requirements](https://webflow.com/certified-partners/apply) need real Webflow client work and an eligible Workspace. There is no verified path from a Next.js portfolio to an immediate Certified Partner listing. The competitor profiles are evidence of the channel, not proof that PandaCodeGen qualifies.
- **Storyblok is achievable after platform work:** Its [listing FAQ](https://www.storyblok.com/faq/how-do-i-become-a-listed-partner-on-storyblok-com) explicitly requires one approved case study. Do not describe a Sanity project as Storyblok experience.

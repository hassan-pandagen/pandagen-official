## Decision

Concentrate on **Sanity and Vercel ecosystem relationships, a Supabase implementation showcase, and one genuinely useful technical resource**. These fit the work the business can demonstrate. Broad directory submissions, paid award entries, and programs for platforms the team does not use should not lead this campaign.

The biggest opportunity visible in this sample is turning delivered work into useful public implementation material. Competitors have links attached to partner relationships, reusable templates, technical explanations and selected design work. A changed company bio does not reproduce those assets or qualifications. This is an inference from the examples below, not a measured explanation of their rankings.

## Which asset best supports the expanded offer

Choose one useful public explanation first, based on evidence already available:

- **Custom software:** a production-sheet reconciliation walkthrough using invented demonstration rows, explaining alias mapping, row accounting and separate production/order states. Our live OPS page supports the real 1,779-row implementation context and states the shared-owner relationship. No claimed time saving is needed.
- **Website/ecommerce builds:** a typed product catalogue example showing how the same registry drives navigation, pages, quote validation and the sitemap. The Emblematic case supplies a relevant implementation story, without pretending that traffic/revenue results have been measured.
- **AI SEO / attribution, later:** a measurement-method article separating captured referrers, customer-declared sources and unknowns, including deduplication and limitations. Resolve the order-source split before pitching the existing combined revenue total as a measurement result. Meta CAPI capability does not prove incremental sales or a standalone service package.

Link an educational resource where a reader needs the explanation. Give that page natural internal links to the corresponding case study and service. Use the portfolio/homepage for agency qualification. Do not try to place every service keyword into an external link anchor or make every backlink land on the homepage.

Do not release client code, customer rows or private CRM screenshots to create an asset. An original synthetic demonstration can explain the engineering without exposing those materials. The proposed tutorials/templates have not been written or published by this audit.

## Defer or exclude

- **CSS-Tricks:** verified competitor link; current article-proposal intake paused.
- **Shopify/Webflow/other CMS directories:** follow genuine platform work and program qualification. Don't buy an unrelated workspace or claim experience for a link.
- **One Page Love:** current agency site is multipage and doesn't meet its one-page submission rule.
- **Awwwards/CSS Design Awards:** optional design promotion with fees, not the first SEO spend. No purchases authorized or made. Keep any paid placements appropriately identified.
- **GitNation sponsorship:** a genuine event/recruitment/community decision with substantial cost; not a backlink acquisition recommendation.
- **Archived university papers citing competitors:** evidence that educational articles attract citations, not a reason to ask libraries to insert agency links into completed theses.
- **Bulk directories, link farms, paid dofollow deals and mandatory reciprocal links:** excluded. Google's [link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) distinguishes manipulative link schemes from ordinary advertising whose links are qualified appropriately.

No dollar budget is assigned to paid placements. Prioritize founder/developer effort on useful work and relevant relationships.

## Coverage and limits

The owner explicitly chose public research for now. Firecrawl fetched specific pages; it is not a backlink index. No traffic estimates, DR/DA scores, full link totals, lost-link history or link-intersection totals were invented. Exact-name searches returning no result mean **not found**, not proven absent. Existing evidence proves presence; no crawler proves that a link never existed.

For a stronger measured gap, capture PandaCodeGen plus competitor referring-domain/page exports with the same source and scope, preserve raw files and dates, then compare active observed domains and verify the source pages. Include brand variants and project domains separately. [Free options and limits](free-tools-and-sender.md) explain a useful starting point without buying a plan.

Public OPS and comparison-page evidence was fetched directly during this audit: [visible text context](captures/public-proof-context.json). The new OPS figures and founder disclosure are now live; the earlier blanket warning that only local files contain them no longer applies. An exact string found in raw HTML can be CSS, script or a withdrawal notice, so raw-match results were not treated as visible claims.

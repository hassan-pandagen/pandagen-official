# AI SEO service page: external demand, search intent and page ownership

Reviewed 14 September 2026. Scope: the supplied [rendered review text](../ai-seo-page-for-review-2026-09-14.md), relevant local service/blog source, earlier keyword research, and fresh external search-result samples. This is an audit and proposed map; no application files were changed.

## Decision

Keep one commercial URL, `/services/ai-seo`, but make its actual service easier to buy before treating the reported keyword volumes as a growth forecast. The draft is clear about a website-building capability and an optional retainer. It is much less clear about the ongoing work a buyer of “AI SEO services” expects to commission. Adding the phrase to metadata does not resolve that difference.

The defensible position is technical implementation for AI-search accessibility, accompanied by the monitoring and changes the business actually offers. A buyer searching for a full-service visibility programme may also expect content production, competitor research, off-site work and business reporting. Either explain the real coverage or acknowledge that the engagement is narrower. Do not silently invent those services to match the keyword.

The strongest existing differentiator is that PandaCodeGen can implement changes in the site and hand over the code and accounts. The strongest unanswered commercial question is: **“What will you actually do on our existing site in the first month, and what will our marketing team receive?”**

The figures warrant a service-offer investigation, not a claim that these are easy customers to acquire:

- DataForSEO calculates difficulty using backlink-derived domain/page scores for the top ten organic results and says the metric is country-specific. KD 0–2 is not a forecast for this website's ranking or conversion rate. [Provider methodology](https://dataforseo.com/help-center/what-is-keyword-difficulty-and-how-is-it-calculated)
- The upstream Google Ads source can combine similar keywords. Do not sum the four volume values into a distinct audience or revenue opportunity. [Provider explanation of grouped volumes](https://dataforseo.com/help-center/sv-broad-exact-phrase-match)
- Missing volume means no value was returned under the recorded settings. It does not establish zero demand or, by itself, a reporting-floor diagnosis. DataForSEO documents other causes, including advertising restrictions; that does not imply these particular service keywords are restricted. [Missing-volume documentation](https://dataforseo.com/help-center/no-search-volume-data-for-some-keywords)
- High CPC is an advertiser-side estimate, not evidence that a visitor will buy PandaCodeGen's particular offer. The reported KD/CPC pairing does not establish how rare the combination is, when the category began, or that difficulty is currently rising.
- There is no UK quantitative capture here. US estimates must not be labelled global or copied into a UK business forecast. A query containing “UK” in the research below is also not a location-controlled UK SERP.

## The important ambiguity: what “AI SEO” means

There are at least two active commercial meanings. WebFX provides an especially clear primary-source example: its [AI SEO service](https://www.webfx.com/seo/services/ai/) describes using AI to support conventional SEO analysis, research and optimisation, while its separate [AI search optimisation service](https://www.webfx.com/seo/services/ai-search-optimization/) describes GEO and visibility in AI-generated answers. Other suppliers combine both in one programme.

The draft mainly addresses the second meaning, and initially only its technical foundation. Consequently, the 1,600/1,300 figures cannot all be assumed to represent people wanting server-rendered content or an AI-readability rebuild. Lead with a plain definition of this company's service, with the acronym as supporting vocabulary.

The same caution applies to short acronyms. `geo agency` can return businesses literally named GEO Agency and directory pages; `aeo services` can return service explanations and provider-selection articles. `AI search` can also mean search functionality inside an ecommerce website. Add the task—visibility in external AI answers—where it prevents confusion.

## What competitors make easier for a mid-size buyer

1Digital's audit page defines a prompt range, competitor count, turnaround, roadmap and deliverable formats, and distinguishes its free technical scan from its paid visibility audit. Those are useful procurement answers. Its claims about exact citation factors and llms.txt should not be copied as established science. [1Digital audit](https://www.1digitalagency.com/aeo-audit/)

eComOne makes the ecommerce audience, technical/content/competitor coverage and report explicit. Its page also contradicts itself on delivery time—five working days near the opening and two in a later answer—so it is an example to learn from selectively. [eComOne audit](https://www.ecomone.com/ai-solutions/ai-visibility-audit/)

For this draft, the missing buyer answers are therefore concrete: assessed pages/templates and markets; what is implemented versus merely reported; who supplies product/content facts; how existing staff or agencies approve changes; which engines are monitored; reporting cadence; what triggers a higher quote; and what is handed over when the retainer ends. These are proposed questions to answer from the actual service, not new commitments being assigned by the audit.

## Cannibalisation: reduced risk is supportable; zero risk is not

The current [topical map](../../../src/data/topical-map.ts) already points the `seo-aeo` cluster to `/services/ai-seo`. The old `/editorial-policy` commercial-destination gap is therefore fixed locally. Do not report it as still open.

The code comment claims this service cannot compete with the eleven posts because they answer informational questions. That is an intended content distinction, not measured proof. The [AEO playbook](../../../src/app/blog/aeo-playbook-built-into-every-website/page.tsx) overlaps the new page's “built into the website” positioning, has `aeo service` in metadata/schema, and discusses practical implementation. The phrase in a meta-keywords tag does not itself cause Google cannibalisation—Google says it ignores that tag—but the shared argument and potential answer coverage warrant clear page roles. [Google on supported metadata](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags)

Keep the service page centred on engagement scope, buyer fit, price, delivery and evidence. Keep the playbook centred on doing or understanding the work. Keep measurement on the existing tracking article. Preserve useful self-canonicals and add contextual service links from the relevant guides; do not canonicalise informational articles to the money page or remove them just because they share vocabulary. Review actual query/page overlap after publication before considering consolidation.

Google's AI-search guidance supports accessible content, internal discovery and matching structured data, and says there are no additional technical requirements or special optimisations for appearing in its AI features. A new acronym page therefore needs commercial utility; it is not a technical admission ticket. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

## Separate pages that are not justified yet

Do not create `/services/aeo`, `/services/geo`, `/services/chatgpt-seo`, `/services/perplexity-seo`, or dozens of country/industry clones to cover synonyms. A supplier having many landing pages is not proof that this is the right architecture for this offer.

A separate **paid AI visibility audit** page can be justified once it has its own purchasable scope, outputs, turnaround, price and distinction from the free checker. A separate **ecommerce AI-search implementation** page can be justified by a real product-data/feed/catalogue offer and supporting delivery evidence. A separate **full-service SEO programme** belongs on another URL only if PandaCodeGen genuinely sells broader content and authority work. None of these should be created to make an unconfirmed scope look complete.

The evidence does not support a ranking promise, a forecast of sales from the four volume figures, or expansion into services the company has not committed to deliver.

## Longer company profile

PandaCodeGen builds custom business websites and online stores, from a first website to the replacement of a site a business has outgrown. We agree what to build, what to keep and what needs to move before work starts.

Website and simple-store builds start at $1,500. Clients work directly with the founders and receive a content editor, training and code they own. The agreed scope determines the final price.

Custom software and integrations are quoted separately. Optional monthly AI SEO services start at $500; ongoing website support and hosting arrangements are scoped separately.

## Contact copy

**Website enquiry:** Ask us about your site

**Compact enquiry:** Ask a question

**Form heading:** What would you like help with?

**Submit:** Send your question

**Reassurance:** A founder replies, usually within one business day. Every message gets an answer, whether or not it turns into a quote.

For a software-specific route, use a label that matches that need, such as **Tell us about your workflow**, leading to an appropriate enquiry. For a real calendar interaction, use **Book a discovery call**. Consistency means accurate expectations, not forcing one string onto every destination.

## Facts that must survive shorter versions

| Fact | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Websites and simple stores from $1,500 | A starting point for a defined entry scope; not every catalogue, migration or integration. |
| Monthly AI SEO from $500 | Optional, separately scoped work; not automatically included in a website build. |
| Content editor and training | Editing the agreed content and functions; new templates or features are separate work. |
| Code ownership | Keep hosting/account arrangements consistent with the actual quote. Do not imply zero provider costs. |
| New builds, redesigns and migrations | A redesign does not automatically require a platform change; a migration does not always require redesign. |
| Founder involvement | Use real roles; do not imply a larger team or different delivery model. |
| Project evidence | Preserve the shared-owner disclosure on affiliated projects and the conditions around reported results. |

Platform names belong in relevant service descriptions, technical profiles or project examples. A short general bio does not need every framework, CMS and source platform. Social biographies should carry the same facts with wording appropriate to their audience.

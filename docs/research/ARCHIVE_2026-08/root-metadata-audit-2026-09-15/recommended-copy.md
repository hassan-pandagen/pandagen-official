# Recommended metadata and company description

Drafted 15 September 2026 from the current company facts. Not applied to application files. These are copy recommendations, not keyword-volume or ranking predictions.

## Root fallback

`src/app/layout.tsx` — retain the `%s | PandaCodeGen` title template and the existing metadata base, indexing directives, icons and verification records.

**Default title**

> Custom Websites & Software | PandaCodeGen

**Default description**

> Custom websites, online stores and software. New builds, redesigns and migrations, with code you own. Work directly with the PandaCodeGen founders.

This names the actual offer and a concrete working relationship. It does not restrict the company to businesses already using particular platforms, does not assign a build price to monthly AI SEO, and does not promise a delivery time. It is a fallback; individual pages should retain descriptions of their own subjects.

## Shared Organization description

Use a single maintained description for the same company entity, including the Organization emitted by the root layout and the duplicate on the homepage. Keeping repeated identity facts in `company-facts.ts` would prevent another partial update.

> PandaCodeGen designs and develops custom business websites, online stores and software. The founders work directly with clients on new builds, redesigns, migrations and integrations. Optional monthly AI SEO and ongoing support are scoped separately.

This is descriptive company information, not a snippet constrained to a particular character count. Preserve the actual legal entity, ownership, address, contact and founding-event distinctions when reconciling the duplicated schema.

## Services directory

The current visible services introduction still says the company rebuilds websites that already earn their keep. Broaden that introduction alongside the directory metadata; changing the search preview alone would preserve the disagreement a buyer encounters after clicking.

**Page title** — let the existing root title template append the brand:

> Website & Software Development Services

**Page description**

> Explore website, ecommerce and software development, redesigns and migrations. Website builds from $1,500; scope and timing agreed before work starts.

**Matching opening**

> We design and build business websites, online stores and custom software. Start with a new build or improve an existing site through a redesign, migration or integration. Scope, price and delivery dates are agreed before work starts.

Use the same scope for the page's Open Graph/Twitter descriptions, CollectionPage schema, social-image copy and introductory heading. This does not require identical wording or changing the URLs of its specialist services.

The $1,500 qualification is specifically for website builds. It does not imply that every software project costs that amount, or replace the separate $500 monthly AI SEO offer.

## Where delivery ranges belong

The current source of truth is explicit:

- Starter: 1–2 weeks.
- Growth: 2–4 weeks.
- Scale: 3–6 weeks.
- Accepted project terms provide the dates for the actual engagement.

Keep those qualified ranges beside their tiers or in a relevant delivery FAQ. Remove the unqualified range from the service-directory meta description. Do not replace it with an unsupported site-wide “average” or a different universal range.

## Scope of the suggested correction

Align the shared company facts and the services directory. Preserve individual migration-page and blog titles/descriptions where they accurately describe that page. Platform names remain useful on relevant service pages; the problem is using them to define the entire business more narrowly than the actual offer.

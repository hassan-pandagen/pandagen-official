# Established competitor benchmark — 15 September 2026

Six established agencies were inspected through their current primary company pages. This is a positioning, buying-journey and evidence-presentation comparison, not an independent verification of their client results. Service pages and case studies describe what each company advertises. A capability or price not found in the inspected pages is **not** evidence that the company lacks it. No lead forms were submitted, and no competitor analytics or private contracts were accessed.

The relevant PandaCodeGen comparison is a founder-delivered studio offering websites and small stores from US$1,500, separately scoped software/integrations, and monthly AI SEO from US$500. These are different engagements from enterprise development teams or integrated marketing programmes. The prices below must not be turned into an equivalent-service savings claim.

## What this means for PandaCodeGen

1. **A broader business can have a focused front door.** These agencies introduce a recognisable problem or service category, then route the buyer into specific services. PandaCodeGen can welcome new websites, redesigns, stores and software without making its hero a list of every integration and acronym. Present Meta CAPI and lead attribution where measurement buyers can find and evaluate them.
2. **Attribution should be a specific, evidenced capability, not a uniqueness claim.** WebFX already advertises CRM-connected attribution, while Hallam's AI Search page discusses pipeline and referral measurement. PandaCodeGen can distinguish its offer through named implementation scope, ownership, verification and price-fit. Do not infer a competitor cannot implement server-side tracking merely because its menu does not say Meta CAPI.
3. **Ownership and content editing are buying criteria, not exclusive inventions.** SmartSites expressly sells both. PandaCodeGen should substantiate its own handover and make the combination of founder access, scoped delivery and usable evidence clear.
4. **Broad proof must still be relevant proof.** A successful store build does not establish paid-ad improvement, a software dashboard does not establish AI SEO outcomes, and AI-referral orders do not prove every assistant can be attributed. Pair each service with the evidence it actually supports.
5. **Do not inflate service parity from headline prices.** A small-site build, a marketing programme and a staffed software engagement are different products. A clear US$1,500 website entry scope and a bounded US$500 monthly AI SEO offer help buyers qualify themselves; claiming those are cheaper versions of these competitors' programmes would need a matched scope.
6. **Proof quality includes limitations, but the buyer must reach the project quickly.** Four sampled named cases show the challenge, actual work and result in an understandable sequence. PandaCodeGen should keep its relationship disclosures and evidence policy accessible, while showing what was built and why the buyer should care before methodological detail.

## Evidence boundaries and observed inconsistencies

- Live primary pages were read on **15 September 2026** using web retrieval. The source inventory is in `established-competitor-sources.json`; it contains research notes and source locations, **not raw downloaded HTML or a private-data export**.
- No search volumes, rankings, conversion rates, backlink metrics or traffic estimates were collected for these competitors. Page structure alone cannot establish that a page ranks or converts well.
- WebFX's 1995/1996 history wording and $8,000/site versus $8,000/month labels are recorded as unresolved differences, not resolved by guesswork.
- Hallam's Hitachi page returned zero-valued animated counters in text extraction. Those were not treated as actual results. Only the narrative results were considered. Its body mixes lab-style speed scores and Core Web Vitals wording; do not reuse that as a model for technical claims.
- Coalition's Native Union body did not substantiate its navigation teaser's percentage figures in the inspected extraction. This is an evidence-presentation limitation of that page, not a finding that the underlying result is false.
- Third-party award/review badges were observed on company pages but were not independently verified in this primary-company-page-only task.
- All company-age statements above rely on the companies' own published histories; they are not corporate-register checks.

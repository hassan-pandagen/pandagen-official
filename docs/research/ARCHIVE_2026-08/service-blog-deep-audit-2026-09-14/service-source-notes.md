## Local evidence

[service-own-source-inventory.json](service-own-source-inventory.json) was generated by reading all `src/app/services/**/PageContent.tsx` files on the research date. It records literal source H1/H2 elements and placement of selected shared components. Comments were removed without changing line positions. The count is of explicit `<section>` tags, not final DOM sections. Dynamic headings and complete shared-component output require manual inspection.

Manually inspected shared sources:

- [CaseStudyGrid](../../../src/components/services/CaseStudyGrid.tsx): data roster, metrics, absence states, visible labels and external link destinations.
- [PricingTiers](../../../src/components/services/PricingTiers.tsx): actual rendered headings rather than ignored call-site props, qualifications, tier values and quote-event CTA.
- [PartnerPromise](../../../src/components/services/PartnerPromise.tsx): repeated commercial/evidence explanation and table.
- [SocialProof](../../../src/components/home/SocialProof.tsx): source-linked reviews, press context and relationship explanation.
- [RelatedServicesGrid](../../../src/components/services/RelatedServicesGrid.tsx): related ordering and number of paths.
- [Service FAQs](../../../src/data/service-faqs.ts): editing boundaries, costs, timing, systems retained and practical objections. Facts were compared internally; this was not an exhaustive platform-documentation verification.
- [Central case facts](../../../src/data/case-study-facts.json): exact relationship and metric statuses. MCP performance is explicitly withdrawn; its hosting figure is permanently retired. Empty/missing metrics for other projects were not treated as proof their hardcoded figures are false.
- [Service metadata](../../../src/app/services/page.tsx), individual service wrappers and [WordPress redirect](../../../src/app/services/wordpress/page.tsx): canonical identity and route purpose.

The earlier [page-design audit](../page-design-audit-2026-09-14/site-page-audit.md) and [agency benchmark](../page-design-audit-2026-09-14/competitor-benchmark.md) provided starting leads. Report findings refer to freshly inspected source or currently retrieved primary pages. The redesigned work index is the available visual/evidence component direction; this report does not revert it to its old presentation or impose the work index's order on every service page.

## Unresolved questions requiring evidence, not invented copy

- The MCP dated delivery-confirmation conflict between the hub and the central record needs its actual underlying record checked.
- Performance labels for Panda Patches, Saforne and Obare need source/method reconciliation before they are used as measured build outcomes.
- Confirm precisely which service enquiries can receive a binding fixed scope/price during the first call; a browser performance test alone does not establish all project requirements.
- Payment-method and subscription transfer feasibility depends on the actual processor and account permissions; the broad WooCommerce FAQ claim should be checked against that delivery process.
- The report does not establish whether the organization has a published Shopify or GHL implementation case beyond the inspected records. No such case is invented for the proposed layout.
- Browser/interaction evidence, screenshots and any subsequent Pagepro rendering results must be documented in the main audit's runtime notes.

## Decision

Use one offer hierarchy across profiles: **custom websites and online stores → custom software and integrations → optional monthly AI SEO**. New builds and redesigns belong in the opening; migrations are a relevant route for an existing site. Explain founder access, content editing, training and code ownership. Website/simple-store pricing starts at US$1,500; software remains quoted by scope; monthly AI SEO starts at US$500 with its own deliverables. Meta CAPI and lead attribution are scoped capabilities, not a newly invented standalone package.

The strongest differentiator supported by the current canon is the combination of a defined small-site starting point, direct founder delivery, client content control and concrete project evidence. Do not turn competitor minimums into an equivalent-service saving or use their technology/industry lists as proof of PandaCodeGen capability.

Recommended shared tagline:

> Custom websites, online stores & software. Work directly with the founders.

The 1,429-character About version is organized around buyer situations and distinct scopes. The short social bio is 142 characters and keeps the two prices attached to the correct services. A description does not need every stack name or every proof metric to be consistent.

## Corrections to the earlier profile-copy note

The earlier [profile-copy-2026-09-16.md](../profile-copy-2026-09-16.md) is now marked superseded:

1. **Texas and Wyoming are different facts.** Principal office in Stafford, Texas and formation in Wyoming are compatible. “Based in Texas” is not itself a false incorporation claim.
2. **Directory budget bands are not exact quotes.** US$1,500 fits a US$1,000–US$10,000 range. Inspect the editor before declaring a coarse bracket contradictory; state the exact starting scope in prose. An hourly-rate field deserves reconciliation with fixed-price delivery, but no replacement rate should be invented.
3. **A company tagline is not a medium paragraph.** The former medium draft was unsuitable for a compact field. New variants have measured character counts and field mappings.
4. **Avoid an accidental software price.** “Websites, stores and software from US$1,500” would assign the website floor to software. The new wording attaches it only to websites/simple stores.
5. **Formatting is not identity.** Equivalent phone/address formats are fine; the underlying name, location and contact details must be accurate. A founded-year field can say 2026 without conflating brand start and incorporation.
6. **G2 is not just reviews.** Its provider description and media are editable brand surfaces that also need review.

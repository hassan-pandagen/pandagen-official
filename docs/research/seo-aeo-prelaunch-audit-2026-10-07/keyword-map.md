# US buyer keyword and page map

**7 October 2026.** These 56 query candidates translate externally observed service categories, competitor coverage and buyer decisions into a publishing plan. They are not exports of PandaCodeGen's queries. Exact wording is proposed; it is not evidence that a particular phrase has a measured search volume. Public searches were not a controlled US rank tracker.

The priorities reflect purchase intent, your existing proof and delivery fit. **P1** means build or improve first, **P2** supports a P1 offer, and **P3** is conditional or primarily useful for technical distribution. No keyword-difficulty or traffic forecast is implied. Several queries intentionally share a destination: this is a map of questions to pages, not a request for 56 new pages.

## First six deliverables

1. **Sanity service page:** queries 1–3, 5 and 7. Sell the configured editor, not just a list of tools. Connect the existing stack guide and independent-client proof.
2. **Backend section on custom engineering:** queries 11–14. Show the whole workflow and a sample scope. A separate Supabase page follows only when it makes the offer clearer.
3. **Upgrade the existing stack guide:** queries 4, 6, 20, 24, 35 and 39. Refresh costs, add the editor/developer responsibility table and link to the commercial offer.
4. **One CMS buyer guide:** queries 17, 21 and initially 22. Compare the same tasks; avoid creating a thin article for every product pair.
5. **One architecture explainer:** queries 18–19. Explain when Sanity, Supabase, both, or neither are needed, with actual scoped examples.
6. **One reusable implementation resource:** choose the moderation or catalogue resource for queries 49–51, based on what can be published and maintained fastest.

For US demand validation, use a US-configured keyword tool or Keyword Planner to check these families and manually inspect the relevant results. Record locale/date, commercial versus tutorial intent, recurring result types and existing page ownership. Use ranges where tools only provide ranges. A phrase with no reported volume can still describe a valuable buyer question, but it should not be sold as a proven traffic opportunity.

Publication gate: every new page must serve a distinct job, have an intended commercial destination, contain evidence or a useful decision aid, and be maintainable. Otherwise improve an existing page or defer it.

## Editing notes

The summary covers a first website, an online store and replacement-site work. Redesign and rebuild are related descriptions, not promises of two separately priced packages. Migration applies when content, URLs or systems need to move. The final quote defines what is preserved, changed or replaced.

“Fast” states the build objective. It should not be expanded into an automatic speed increase, a universal one-second target or unchanged search rankings. Keep the documented acceptance conditions on the pricing and service pages.

Do not repeat the full platform list in the H1, hero lead, summary and service introduction. The lower section gives those names a practical role. Retain Shopify or GoHighLevel where the agreed architecture calls for it; the links must not imply that all underlying platforms are removed.

Keep the existing **Ask us about your site** and **See our work** actions. This short summary does not need another large CTA between the hero and the projects. Detailed pricing, timelines, optional support and performance checks remain in their appropriate sections.

The source summary currently links **web development** to an agency-facing blog article. For this broad buyer journey, use a relevant commercial service link where it describes the destination accurately. The existing `/services` page owns general redesign/migration detail; `/services/ecommerce` owns store work. Do not create duplicate service pages merely to place a keyword.

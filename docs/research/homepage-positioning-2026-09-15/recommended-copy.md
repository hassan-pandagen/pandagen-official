## Delivery and ownership

You'll work directly with founders Hassan and Imran. We agree what needs to be built, review the work with you and prepare your team to run the finished site.

1. **Understand the job.** Discuss the customers, content and daily tasks the site needs to support.
2. **Agree the scope.** Set out the pages, features, responsibilities, price and delivery plan.
3. **Build and review.** Check the design, content and working features against that plan.
4. **Launch and hand over.** Complete the agreed checks, hand over the code, arrange the agreed account access and train your team on the editor.

## Buyer questions

**Can you build a new site, or only replace an existing one?**

Both. We can build from a new brief or plan the replacement of your current site, including the content and connections that need to carry across.

**Can our team update the website?**

Yes. The agreed content editor lets your team update the content it covers. We document it and train you. New layouts or functionality are scoped separately.

**Who owns the finished work?**

At handover, you receive the source code, design files, CMS models and documentation. Your quote sets out account and hosting arrangements. If we host on our account, hosting can move to your account on request or when the engagement ends.

**What will it cost to run?**

Hosting and connected services depend on the setup. We identify the expected costs and responsibilities in the quote. Ongoing support is optional and separately priced.

**Can you keep our search traffic when replacing the site?**

We plan redirects, content and technical checks to reduce avoidable migration risks. Search engines control rankings, so we cannot promise unchanged traffic. The project plan records the controls and launch monitoring.

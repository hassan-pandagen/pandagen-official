# Agency page benchmark — 14 September 2026

This benchmark supports PandaCodeGen's work-page redesign and the wider page audit. It examines six agencies with relevant published work: Bejamas, FocusReactive, Naturaily, 10up/Fueled, Human Made, and Clay. The first three overlap closely with headless development and replatforming; 10up and Human Made provide strong enterprise migration examples; Clay is a presentation reference for website design. This is a selected comparison, not a ranking by revenue, size, or conversion performance.

**The strongest common pattern is specific work presented as a client story:** who the client is, what needed to change, what the agency delivered, what visitors can inspect, and what happened afterwards. Panda should make those answers easier to see before adding more capability claims.

## Method and limits

Reviewed primary agency websites through web search and page extraction on 14 September 2026. Most observations below concern accessible headings, content order, images identified in the page, links, and labels. A follow-up also captured and visually inspected **Bejamas and Clay's work indexes at 1440 × 1000**, at the top and 900 pixels down the page. The scoped desktop findings are recorded below. Mobile layouts, hover behavior, animation quality, full-page behavior and contrast measurements were not tested. Other agency pages remain content/structure observations. An image-led design for Panda is a recommendation, not a claim that every reference uses an identical grid.

Competitor outcome figures are agency-published claims; their measurement datasets were not independently audited. A polished portfolio does not validate its statistics. Search-cached content can also lag the live site. Access failures are recorded below rather than interpreted as missing pages.

## Six reference agencies

### 1. Bejamas — closest migration and headless comparison

- **Work index:** [Selected projects](https://bejamas.com/work). Entries combine the client, a business change, technologies, and selected reported outcomes. The substantial archive offers service, technology, and industry filters. Several project descriptions emphasize publishing autonomy rather than framework adoption alone.
- **Case study:** [Bennetts](https://bejamas.com/work/bennetts). The page identifies the year, services and stack; links to the live website; includes desktop/mobile interface images; organizes the account around challenges and solutions; attributes a testimonial to a named client role; and ends with contact and next-project paths. Its reported performance figures are not independently verified here.
- **Service page:** [Website migration](https://bejamas.com/services/website-migration). The page offers section links for scope, process, search continuity, pricing and FAQs. It separates platform, frontend, content and URL work, then discusses integration, training and handover. A named migration-client testimonial appears within the service explanation.

**Apply to Panda:** Describe the change in ordinary business language before listing the stack. On a small portfolio, a featured project and a short grid are sufficient; Bejamas's multi-axis filters solve an archive-size problem Panda should not imitate prematurely. A migration page should make the actual work and launch responsibilities inspectable.

### 2. FocusReactive — explicit project scope and engineering ownership

- **Work showcase:** [Homepage featured work](https://focusreactive.com/). The inspected showcase distinguishes industry, technical stack and scope of work, with project images and case/live links. This supplies useful context even before opening a full story. A separate complete work index could not be verified during this pass.
- **Case study:** [Arrive](https://focusreactive.com/arrive-case-study/). The page includes a live link, client challenges, delivered components and content structures, solution details, named engineering team members, other projects and an enquiry form. There are outcome claims, but their original evidence is not exposed by this review. The homepage and case page also describe the CMS stack differently; use the presentation pattern without treating every detail as reconciled.
- **Service page:** [Next.js development](https://focusreactive.com/next-js-expert-agency/). The offer separates new builds, modernization and ongoing support. It connects its engineering approach to maintainability and handover, includes a recent project, and discusses architecture choices in the context of business requirements.

**Apply to Panda:** Use a short, accurate role line for each project: migration, design, implementation, integration or ongoing work, as supported by its record. Place technology underneath. Engineering pages need concrete handover artifacts and a relevant example, not only a list of framework features.

### 3. Naturaily — buyer tasks and delivery outputs

- **Work index:** [Portfolio](https://naturaily.com/portfolio) is linked from the current navigation, but its extraction exceeded the tool's size limit. The inspected [homepage showcase](https://naturaily.com/) pairs client names and industries with project summaries and selected outputs, such as migrated content and custom search, alongside claimed results.
- **Case study:** [FGS Global](https://naturaily.com/portfolio/fgs-global). The introduction summarizes the platform problem and delivered system. Subsequent sections explain requirements, legacy constraints, technical responsibility, integrations and the delivery team. Client quotations are attributed by name and role, and a live-site link appears in the project facts. Some extracted animated counters show zero; this review therefore uses the written narrative, not those counter values.
- **Service page:** [Website redesign](https://naturaily.com/services/website-redesign-company). The page distinguishes a focused redesign from a redesign with replatforming, explains what the agency does and what the client receives at each stage, and links related project examples. It also contains an unrelated real-estate enquiry heading: a useful reminder that established competitors can have template mistakes.

**Apply to Panda:** Give each service a clear fit boundary and tangible outputs. A visitor should understand whether they need design, a migration, or engineering help before comparing platforms. Keep the useful clarity while making Panda's own page considerably easier to scan.

### 4. 10up / Fueled — evidence through detailed role attribution

10up is now the name of Fueled's WordPress practice, rather than a separate agency in this comparison. The change was explained in its [May 2025 announcement](https://10up.com/blog/2025/new-fueled-brand-10up-becomes-wordpress-practice/).

- **Work index:** [10up's published portfolio](https://10up.com/our-work/) labels projects with a client and a recognizable engagement type: migration, rebuild, design or support. This is useful information architecture even without assessing the visual layout.
- **Case study:** [EAB](https://10up.com/our-work/eab/). It explicitly credits another firm for the initial design and explains 10up's implementation, migration and Salesforce responsibilities. It distinguishes the client's content work and architect's contribution, includes component and interface imagery, describes training/UAT, and attributes post-launch observations to a defined first-month period. This is a strong model for explaining collaboration without taking credit for everything.
- **Service page:** [Fueled's 10up WordPress practice](https://fueled.com/wordpress/). The page uses actual projects and named integrations to demonstrate capabilities, then distinguishes ongoing partnership, staff augmentation and maintenance. Its [expertise overview](https://fueled.com/expertise/) groups work by strategy, design, build, growth and managed services.

**Apply to Panda:** Separate outcomes from scope and credit other contributors. Agency-partner work must state Panda's actual part. Link specific services to the most relevant example rather than showing the same generic proof strip everywhere.

### 5. Human Made — migration as organizational change

- **Work index:** [Our work](https://humanmade.com/work/). Search-cached primary content describes projects through recognizable changes, including migrations, editorial workflows, performance and redesign.
- **Case study:** [Capgemini](https://humanmade.com/work/a-drupal-to-wordpress-migration-for-capgemini/). The inspected cached page identifies industry, services and contributors, explains the publishing needs behind the move, and attributes a client quotation to a named director. It discusses a component library and editorial workflows, then offers a deeper downloadable account. Direct page fetches returned 403, so this is a cached-content observation.
- **Service page:** [Drupal migration service example](https://humanmade.com/expertise/drupal-7-end-of-life-migrations/). The cached page places a relevant Capgemini story beside the offer, outlines migration stages including training, and explains what a consultation covers. Its time-sensitive Drupal 7 end-of-life copy is stale for September 2026 and should not be copied.

**Apply to Panda:** Show why the client needed the change and how their team operates after handover. Keep core case-study evidence available on the page; a download can supplement it. Use the training and responsibility detail, not the outdated urgency.

### 6. Clay — concise commissions and visible deliverables

- **Work index:** [Clay's work](https://clay.global/work). Entries pair a client with a concise description of the commission and discipline tags. It provides categories for a large archive, and labels case studies, live websites and unavailable stories differently.
- **Case study:** [Marqeta](https://clay.global/work/marqeta). The account establishes the commission and disciplines, then explains the card-based narrative, interaction choices, inner pages and responsive implementation. Interface images accompany the explanation. It does not need a numerical growth claim to communicate design decisions and delivered work.
- **Service page:** [Services](https://clay.global/services). Capabilities are grouped into branding, products, websites, content and development, with concrete disciplines under each. The page also relates its work to client industries and closes with direct contact.

**Apply to Panda:** Use real website imagery as the main portfolio content, with restrained labels and a clear story. Distinguish a case-study link from a live-site link. Do not invent a case study or business result to make every card identical.

## Recommended page structures for Panda

These are proposed structures, not measured conversion prescriptions.

| Page | Visitor's main question | Recommended sequence |
|---|---|---|
| Work index | Have you built something relevant, and does it look good? | Short introduction; strongest documented project with a generous real preview; remaining projects with client, task, role and preview; one invitation to discuss a similar project. |
| Individual case study | What was your contribution, and can I believe the result? | Client and problem; live preview and role; project facts; the relevant before/after or delivered screens; decisions and constraints; outcomes with measurement context; client attribution; related service and contact. |
| Service overview | Which kind of help do I need? | Plain-language choices by buyer task; concise fit and deliverables; relevant work beside each major offer; process/engagement summary; next step. |
| Migration service | What moves, what changes, and how is the launch handled? | Fit and destination; real migration example; agreed content/integration scope; client inputs and approvals; redirect/QA/cutover process; price and start conditions; exclusions and ongoing responsibilities; FAQ and contact. |
| Custom engineering / agency partnership | Can you take responsibility for our technical scope? | Type of engagement; exact role; relevant integration or implementation proof; collaboration model and artifacts; scope/pricing approach; ownership/support; qualified enquiry. |
| About / contact | Who will deliver, and what happens after I enquire? | Named people and roles with actual credentials; how the engagement works; relevant proof; concise contact choices and truthful response expectations. |

The work index should be primarily a portfolio. Put long technical methodology, metric qualifications and commercial details on the relevant case or service page, while retaining enough context on each card to avoid misleading the buyer. Outcome numbers are optional; accurate scope, usable screenshots and a credible explanation are not interchangeable with them.

For Panda's redesign, use its existing real project assets. Make the project image large enough to inspect, avoid decorative dashboards that compete with it, keep text outside the screenshot, and use a consistent card structure. These are implementation choices for the current portfolio, not claims about competitor conversion rates.

## Evidence rules for the broader audit

1. **One source of truth for each project.** Client name, role, technologies, image, URL, service mapping and published outcomes should agree across cards and case pages.
2. **A screenshot proves appearance, not business impact.** Identify what it depicts. Performance, traffic and revenue claims need their own dates, method and source.
3. **Show delivery, not just a promise.** A redirect map excerpt, component system, editing workflow or integration diagram can support a migration/engineering story when it comes from the actual project and can be shared.
4. **Carry commercial boundaries into the page.** Scope, price assumptions, client inputs, acceptance criteria, hosting and ongoing work should be understandable before the enquiry.
5. **Benchmark critically.** Do not adopt competitor traffic guarantees, unexplained scores, stale urgency, or claims about AI visibility simply because a polished agency publishes them.

## Access notes

Pagepro was considered because its positioning overlaps closely with Panda's. Its homepage and case-study index returned 403 in this pass; accessible blog content is not a substitute for inspecting those page types, so it is not counted as a completed benchmark. Human Made observations are explicitly limited to search-cached primary content. FocusReactive's homepage work showcase was inspected, but guessed work-index URLs were not verified. Naturaily's portfolio URL was discovered from its navigation; only its homepage showcase and individual case/service pages were fully extractable.

No competitor page was copied into Panda, and no competitor result is presented as a Panda result. The practical direction is to make Panda's real work more visible and its actual role easier to understand.

## Desktop visual observations

A first sandboxed attempt failed with `net::ERR_NETWORK_ACCESS_DENIED`. A subsequent run with network access succeeded: both pages returned HTTP 200, and the visible images reported loaded. Four screenshots were inspected, covering each page's top and a position 900 pixels down, at 1440 × 1000 with reduced motion. No forms were submitted. The successful capture log is `audit/work-redesign-2026-09-14/competitor-visual-qa.json`.

**Bejamas:** A pale background and large centered heading leave substantial space before a thin divider and inline filters. The initial projects use large, rounded, unequal-width image areas; small technology labels sit above them and descriptive captions below. The imagery mixes brand covers and a website shown on a phone. Further down, another card is offset to the right, and a compact floating filter strip is visible. This is an intentionally sparse portfolio composition rather than a uniform wall of text panels. [Inspected work page](https://bejamas.com/work).

**Clay:** The white page has a large left-aligned two-line headline and a compact category list to its right. One broad featured image dominates the first project; its client name and short commission description appear underneath. The following two images are staggered vertically with generous separation. Color comes mainly from the project imagery, while the page typography and navigation stay restrained. The initial feature is a brand artwork, so this reference supports visual prominence and hierarchy, not a rule that every cover must be a website screenshot. [Inspected work page](https://clay.global/work).

**Implication for Panda:** Use a calm page canvas, a clear featured project, large consistent previews, and a small amount of outside-the-image text. Panda can retain a simpler grid and smaller spacing than these larger archives. Its actual website screenshots are particularly useful because buyers need to inspect the kind of site Panda will deliver. The reference is the balance of imagery and explanation, not the exact artwork or layout.

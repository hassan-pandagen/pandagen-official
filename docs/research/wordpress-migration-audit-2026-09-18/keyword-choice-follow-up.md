## Recommended coverage

- **Title/H1:** WordPress to Next.js Migration Services.
- **Scope heading:** What our WordPress migration service includes.
- **Architecture:** explain replacing WordPress, the scoped editor and what keeping wp-admin would require.
- **Useful long-tail questions:** content/blog migration; Elementor/Divi layouts; custom fields and plugin functions; URLs/SEO checks; editing; cost; timeline. Mention supported delivery, without asserting measured demand for every variant.
- **Commerce:** show Panda Patches' operating system. Explain that checkout, product/order data and integrations affect scope and price.

The broad language can occur naturally alongside the destination-specific offer. No new page or keyword-stuffed title is needed. Keep the existing URL, instructional guide and cost guide.

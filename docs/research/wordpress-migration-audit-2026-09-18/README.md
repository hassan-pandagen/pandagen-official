# WordPress migration service: complete audit and action plan

**18 September 2026.** Page: https://www.pandacodegen.com/services/wordpress-migration. Original repository baseline: `0b1b8251f74032e4fecc03bfc501ef17da9a3483`. The original audit was read-only. A subsequent scoped repository revision implements project proof, payment history and delivery-status corrections; see [implementation follow-up](implementation-follow-up.md). Baseline findings below are historical observations, not a claim that every finding remains in the current working tree. The broader offer, metadata and keyword recommendations remain pending.

## Decision

Prioritize this existing service page. Its offer fits a specific hiring query, and the business has relevant migration experience to show. The present page makes a buyer work too hard to find that experience, understand the scope and start a useful conversation. Its long anti-WordPress argument also contains contradictions that undermine the careful evidence work elsewhere on the site.

The recommended change is one coordinated revision: an explicit service opening, relevant case study, clear CMS/editor decision, scope and price, migration controls, focused questions and a working enquiry path. Keep the existing URL and informational cluster. Treat top five as a measured objective; this audit establishes neither a ranking guarantee nor a single proven cause of the historical position.

**Owner follow-up, September 18:** lead with live, founder-affiliated Panda Patches and the live independent-client Ladies 4 Jesus migration. A Step Above Medical may appear as **in progress, launch pending**, with no completed-work image, result or case-study link. MyCustomPatches remains supporting independent delivery evidence later on the page. The owner confirmed Panda Patches started with Stripe and later moved to Square, its current provider, and confirmed Ladies 4 Jesus's WordPress origin. Greater exact-term monthly volume for the proposed keyword has not been established; its recommendation is based on service intent. See [keyword comparison](keyword-choice-follow-up.md), [Panda Patches proof](panda-patches-proof.md) and [implemented scope](implementation-follow-up.md).

## Recommended page flow

1. **Offer, entry price and founder enquiry.** Keep the main action reachable without an oversized paragraph/heading combination.
2. **Live migration work, with status made explicit.** Lead with Panda Patches, the founder-affiliated WordPress-to-Next.js operating business: Sanity editor, custom pricing, checkout and connected order workflow. State Stripe first, Square now. Beside it, show the independent Ladies 4 Jesus WordPress migration and owner-managed publishing/moderation. Use real existing screenshots and permanent case links. Below those live cases, A Step Above Medical is a text-only **in-progress** project; launch is pending and no results are claimed. Keep MyCustomPatches as supporting independent evidence later on the page. Its 200+ URLs, 13 templates and about 22 days belong only to that project and are not a promise for every engagement. A real editor capture remains a useful future addition; a storefront screenshot does not prove the editing workflow.
3. **What moves and what gets rebuilt.** A compact scope table for content/media, templates, custom fields, plugin functions, URLs/metadata, forms/integrations and editorial workflow. Mark complex commerce, membership and multilingual requirements as separately scoped where appropriate.
4. **What happens to WordPress and editing.** Explain the priced full-replacement offer, the chosen editor and retained-WordPress distinction. Show an actual editor example with capabilities bounded to the content model.
5. **Price and ongoing costs.** Current Starter/Growth/Scale ranges, content-count rules and what changes a quote. Hosting, CMS/provider fees and optional support are separate agreed items; no blanket zero-cost claim.
6. **Migration controls and delivery.** Inventory, mapping, staging review, acceptance checks, launch/rollback plan, and the monitoring/support actually included. Keep unchanged URLs where appropriate; redirect changed URLs deliberately. Ranking changes remain possible. [Google migration guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes)
7. **Focused FAQ and one close.** Cover editing, existing design, Elementor/Divi/custom fields, commerce, ongoing maintenance, realistic timelines and SEO risk. Link to the cost, implementation and SEO-risk guides where useful.

A shorter page is the likely result of removing repeated arguments, not a target word count. Keep evidence qualifications next to the claims they qualify. The old MyCustomPatches performance measurements stay withdrawn and its historical hosting-cost claim stays retired. A new test can support a new dated observation, not reconstruct an old baseline.

## Technical and authority assessment

The direct live capture returned 200, self-canonical, index/follow, accessible robots rules, sitemap inclusion, one H1, content in initial HTML, and five FAQ entries matching the visible answers. No page exceptions, broken fragment targets, horizontal overflow or failed loaded images were observed in the bounded checks. **No basic crawl exclusion was found.** This is not a URL Inspection result or a field-performance test. Details and screenshots: [live browser verification](live-browser-check.md).

Do not sell additional FAQ schema as a route to richer Google snippets: Google's current changelog says FAQ rich results stopped appearing on May 7, 2026. Keep useful FAQs for buyers, and keep any retained structured data accurate. [Google Search updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates#may-2026)

The sitemap date should reflect a real content change. Raising its priority or changing its frequency is not a ranking tactic; Google ignores those two values. [Sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)

The owner's preserved Links report shows 247 of 249 reported incoming links going to the homepage and two to a headless-commerce article. This service is not listed in that sample; that does **not** prove it has zero backlinks. [Preserved owner report](../backlink-outreach-2026-09-16/captures/gsc-top-linking-sites-owner-supplied-2026-09-17.json). Google describes the Links report's limitations in its [documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9049606?hl=en).

After the page and proof are corrected, the useful outreach asset is the documented migration case or a real, permissioned/anonymized migration artifact. A relevant client/vendor/community editorial mention can help people inspect the work. Founder-owned links and your own syndicated posts should remain classified as such. ## Release and measurement plan

**Before release:** retain this snapshot; export the exact query with its Pages table, then the service URL's Queries table for the latest complete 28 days and the preceding 28. Preserve dates, Web search type, country and device filters. Use the older 92-day archive as historical context, not a mismatched short-window control. Confirm headless availability, package content-count rules and the actual monitoring/support commitment before writing those into the offer.

**One scoped release:** update the service, relevant shared FAQ, truthful proof presentation and enquiry behavior together. Preserve URL/canonical and article roles. Use the current company/project facts; centralize repeated commercial values. If shared components change, check their other consumers. Run the repository's normal check/build/guards and focused browser tests for FAQ, enquiry, anchors and both viewport layouts. Record deployed SHA/time and re-capture live HTML. Request recrawl once if appropriate; repeated requests are not a ranking plan.

**First week:** inspect live status/canonical, rendered title/description, enquiry errors and Google-selected canonical. Recheck whether the desired query is showing this page. Fix functional defects immediately.

**Following weeks:** review weekly for faults, but evaluate a complete 28-day window and, at this low query volume, a longer 56-day window if needed. Track the same service/agency query family, page-level clicks, CTR, average position, relevant enquiries and accepted projects separately. Country/device splits may have tiny samples; retain totals and counts. Do not claim that a simultaneous copy, proof and CTA revision isolates any one factor.

For a useful top-five target, define the intended country/device and this URL first. Report GSC average position alongside repeated local SERP observations and commercial outcomes, rather than treating one personalized search as success. If the tutorial owns the service query, distinguish and connect the service before considering any consolidation. If the service gains impressions but no clicks, inspect the live result/title against that search; if clicks rise but enquiries do not, inspect fit and form completion. If neither improves, revisit competition and independent proof before another cosmetic headline change.

Unresolved evidence: current joint query/page export, Google-selected canonical, field Core Web Vitals, complete competitor backlink gap, and authenticated raw historical client performance records. These limits do not prevent the specific buyer/accuracy fixes above, but they prevent a defensible prediction of when or whether top five will follow.

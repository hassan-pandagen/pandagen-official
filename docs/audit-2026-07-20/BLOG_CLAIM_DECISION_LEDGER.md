## What FAIL means

**FAIL is an internal publishing-quality verdict. It is not evidence that Google has applied a manual action, algorithmic penalty, or ranking suppression to the route.** The audit did not establish a Google penalty for any article. A route can rank while containing a claim that still needs better evidence, qualification, contract alignment, or clearer attribution.

No row below authorizes deletion, shortening, merging, redirecting, retitling, loss of query-bearing headings, or removal of useful topical coverage. Repair each disputed portion in place. Preserve the route, search intent, useful depth, internal links, and GSC equity. If a risky paragraph is reduced, replace it with equally useful verified explanation, methodology, limitations, or decision guidance.

The recommendation codes are deliberately narrow:

- **KEEP**: retain the claim as written because the repository already records adequate owner confirmation. This does not prevent improving nearby attribution.
- **SOURCE**: retain the substance only after attaching dated primary or owner evidence that proves the exact claim.
- **QUALIFY**: preserve the idea but state its limits, assumptions, measurement context, or non-causal nature.
- **REWRITE**: replace the public wording because it conflicts with the current scope-by-contract model, combines incompatible claim types, or uses a categorical framing that evidence alone would not make responsible.

## Owner review workflow

For each row, the owner can choose one of three safe paths without losing article depth:

1. Supply the evidence listed in the final column. The claim can remain if the evidence proves its exact scope and the article attributes it accurately.
2. Confirm that the statement was intended as an estimate, scenario, opinion, or project-specific observation. Reword only that statement and add its assumptions or boundary.
3. Confirm that no evidence exists or that the public promise is broader than intended. Replace the disputed text with useful methodology, decision criteria, limitations, examples, or a scoped invitation to discuss the project.

The repair pass must keep a before-and-after word count for every article and must not reduce an article by more than 5% without explicit owner approval. Updating a visible FAQ answer also requires updating its shared FAQ source so the rendered FAQ and FAQ structured data remain identical.
